## Transformation

The original extraction did not preserve a submission identifier per
row. Contiguous source segments were enumerated as candidate
assignments to the two public submissions. Per-cell coverage
constraints and all 18 published scored cell accuracies retained
exactly one assignment. The corresponding public IDs were mapped onto
the previously unlabeled rows.

The reconstruction also restores 326 independently re-extracted rows
for `EQT01-T00458`: GPT-OSS normal problems 1--126 and Gemma normal
problems 1--200. Rows retained from each source artifact form
contiguous, non-overlapping source segments. The released dataset is
sorted by `submission_id`, `board`, `model`, `problem_set`, and
`problem_index`; every resulting group is contiguous.

| Stage | Candidates |
|---|---:|
| Raw row-count constraints | 11,440 |
| Per-cell constraints | 128 |
| Official-accuracy validation | 1 |

Results are regenerated by:

```bash
python3 scripts/validate_matrix_reconstruction.py
```

## Encoding

- `result`: `correct`, `incorrect`, `unparsed`, or `mixed`.
  `unparsed` (`U`) counts as not correct in accuracy calculations.
- `runs`: `G`, `R`, or `U` denotes three identical runs in a compressed
  unanimous cell. A three-character value records mixed per-run
  outcomes in DOM order.

## Scope

The reconstruction maps public submission IDs onto rows that originally
lacked them and restores the 326 independently recovered outcomes. No
leaderboard score, preserved problem outcome, model output, or
evaluation result was altered.
